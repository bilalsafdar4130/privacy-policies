Privacy Policy — Pitch Alchemy

Last updated: 24 September 2026 · Package com.pitchalchemy.game

Pitch Alchemy (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a 5-a-side cage football game, developed and published by Enigvra Studio. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it goes, and how you delete it.

The short version.
  • There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking in the app.
  • Your player, your career and your settings are stored only on your device.
  • Local multiplayer talks only to phones on the same Wi‑Fi network as you — never to a server of ours.
  • There are no real-money purchases. The shop uses in-game coins only.

What the app stores, on your device only

InformationWhy
Your player (name you type, appearance, position, skills) and your career, league and match historySo your player and progress are there next time.
A random player ID and a recovery phraseSo a local match can recognise you if your connection drops, and so you can move your player to a new phone. They are generated on your phone and are not linked to you.
Your settings (sound, controls, graphics)To remember your choices.

Local multiplayer

When you host or join a match on your local network, the app announces the game on that network and exchanges, with the other players in the match only: your player name, your player’s appearance and stats, your random player ID, and the live match (positions, passes, scores). This goes directly between the phones on your Wi‑Fi. We never receive it, and it is not stored anywhere except in each player’s own match history.

Permissions

The app asks for network access only, so phones on the same Wi‑Fi can find each other and play. It does not ask for location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.

What we never collect

No email, no real name (the player name is whatever you type), no advertising ID, no analytics and no crash reports.

Children

The app has no chat, no advertising and no purchases, and shares nothing outside your local network. It is suitable for all ages; younger players should play local matches with people they know.

Delete your data

In the app: open Settings → Privacy & data and tap Delete my data. It erases your player, career, match history, player ID, recovery phrase and settings, and restarts the game fresh.

What it cannot delete: a copy of your player name and stats that another phone on your network kept in its own match history — that is on their device. We hold nothing.

By email: if you cannot open the app, email bilalsafdar3014@gmail.com with the subject “Pitch Alchemy data deletion”. Because we hold no copy of your data, the answer will explain how to remove it from your device; we reply within 30 days and will never ask for a password.

Uninstalling the app also deletes everything it stored on your device.

Changes to this policy

If what the app collects ever changes — for example if it ever adds analytics, advertising or an online service — this page is updated before that version is released, and the date at the top changes with it.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or about anything above: bilalsafdar3014@gmail.com
