Privacy Policy — Pitch Alchemy

Last updated: 8 October 2026 · Package com.pitchalchemy.game

Pitch Alchemy (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a 5-a-side cage football game, developed and published by Enigvra Studio. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it goes, and how you delete it.

The short version.
  • There are no accounts, no analytics and no tracking in the app, and this version shows no advertising. Future versions may show ads from Google AdMob or AppLovin — see Advertising and your ad choices.
  • Your player, your career and your settings are stored only on your device.
  • Local multiplayer talks only to phones on the same Wi‑Fi network as you — never to a server of ours.
  • There are no real-money purchases. The shop uses in-game coins only.

What the app stores, on your device only

InformationWhy
Your player (name you type, appearance, position, skills) and your career, league and match historySo your player and progress are there next time.
A random player ID and a recovery phraseSo a local match can recognise you if your connection drops, and so you can move your player to a new phone. They are generated on your phone and are not linked to you.
Your settings (sound, controls, graphics)To remember your choices.

Local multiplayer

When you host or join a match on your local network, the app announces the game on that network and exchanges, with the other players in the match only: your player name, your player’s appearance and stats, your random player ID, and the live match (positions, passes, scores). This goes directly between the phones on your Wi‑Fi. We never receive it, and it is not stored anywhere except in each player’s own match history.

Permissions

The app asks for network access only, so phones on the same Wi‑Fi can find each other and play. It does not ask for location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.

What we never collect

No email, no real name (the player name is whatever you type), no advertising ID, no analytics and no crash reports.

Advertising and your ad choices

This version of Pitch Alchemy shows no advertising and contains no advertising SDK. Enigvra Studio plans to support its apps with advertising, so this section already describes how advertising works in any version of Pitch Alchemy that shows it. That version will not be released until this page names it, with the date at the top updated.

In a version that shows advertising:

  • Who serves the ads. Google AdMob, which may also fill an ad slot through mediation with AppLovin (AppLovin MAX) and the ad networks they work with. Ad content is limited to a general-audience rating.
  • What an ad network receives. Your device's advertising ID (a resettable identifier Android provides; the app declares the AD_ID permission for it), your IP address (from which the network infers an approximate location such as your country or city), technical information about your device and the app, and how each ad performed — whether it loaded, was shown or was tapped. We never give an ad network your name, email address, contacts, precise location or anything you type.
  • The advertising ID is kept apart from analytics. It is read for advertising only and is never joined to the app's own statistics.
  • Europe, the UK and Switzerland: consent first. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the app shows Google's consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform, a Google-certified consent management platform that uses the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework) before any ad is requested. No ad is requested and the advertising ID is not read until you have answered. Ads are personalised only if you agree to personalisation; otherwise any ad you see is non-personalised, chosen from the app and the screen it appears on.
  • United States: opt out of targeted advertising. Where a US state privacy law gives you the right to opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information or of targeted advertising, Google's privacy form offers that choice; see US state privacy notice.
  • Change your answer in the app at any time, from the app's privacy settings, wherever Google's form applies to you.
  • Anywhere in the world you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your phone's own settings (usually Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads). Ads still appear, but they are not based on a profile of you.
  • Children. No personalised advertising is shown to anyone we know to be under 13.

Each network uses the information under its own privacy policy: How Google uses information from apps that use its services · Google Privacy Policy · AppLovin Privacy Policy.

Where data is processed

Google (AdMob), AppLovin process data on servers in the United States and other countries. This version sends nothing to them; this applies to any version that shows advertising. Transfers from the EEA, the UK and Switzerland rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework (and its UK and Swiss extensions) where the recipient is certified under it, and otherwise on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or an equivalent safeguard. Enigvra Studio itself runs no server that receives your data.

How long data is kept

  • On your device: until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app.
  • Advertising data is kept by each ad network under its own retention policy. Google, for example, anonymises advertising data in its logs by removing part of the IP address after 9 months and cookie information after 18 months.
  • Emails you send us are kept only as long as we need them to answer you, and deleted on request.

Delete your data

In the app: open Settings → Privacy and tap Delete my data. It erases your player, career, match history, player ID, recovery phrase and settings, and restarts the game fresh.

What it cannot delete: a copy of your player name and stats that another phone on your network kept in its own match history — that is on their device. We hold nothing.

By email: if you cannot open the app, email bilalsafdar3014@gmail.com with the subject “Pitch Alchemy data deletion”. Because we hold no copy of your data, the answer will explain how to remove it from your device; we reply within 30 days and will never ask for a password.

Uninstalling the app also deletes everything it stored on your device.
