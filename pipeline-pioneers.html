Privacy Policy — Pipeline Pioneers

Last updated: 8 October 2026 · Package com.bilalsafdar.pipelinepioneers

Pipeline Pioneers (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a puzzle game for Android in which you build a CPU from a single logic gate up to a program you wrote. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, who it is shared with, and what control you have over it.

The short version.
  • There is no account. You never give us a name, an email address or a phone number, because there is nowhere to type one.
  • The app shows one banner advertisement on the menu screens (never inside a lab), served by Google AdMob. There are no full-screen or video adverts.
  • In-app purchases are optional: AD-FREE WORKSHOP and PIONEER SUPPORTER, each a single, one-time payment through Google Play Billing. Nothing that teaches is ever for sale: every lesson, lab and hint is free.
  • The app collects anonymous usage statistics through Google Firebase Analytics.
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google’s consent form asks you before advertising or statistics are switched on.
  • Your saved progress lives on your device. It is never uploaded.
  • Advertising is served by Google AdMob, and may also be served through AppLovin — see Advertising and your ad choices.

What the app collects

Anonymous usage statistics (Firebase Analytics)

The app reports events describing what happens in it — a level opened, a circuit completed, a chip unlocked, a setting changed, or an error — so that we can tell which parts people enjoy and which parts are broken.

What is sentExample
Events and their parametersthe screen opened, how long a session lasted, an error
The app version and platformthe version number, Android

Coarse information Google’s SDK addscountry, language, device model, OS version, and the fact that the app was opened

The app is set so that Firebase Analytics does not collect your advertising ID.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

To show and measure the banner advertisement, Google AdMob receives:

  • your device’s advertising ID (the app declares the AD_ID permission for this);
  • your IP address, from which Google infers an approximate location such as your country or city;
  • technical information about your device and how the advertisement performed (whether it loaded, was shown or was tapped).

Depending on your settings and where you live, Google may use this to show advertising that is relevant to you. Advertising content is limited to a general-audience rating.

What the app does not collect

No name, email address, phone number, postal address, contacts, calendar, photos, files, microphone, camera, precise location, browsing or search history, list of installed apps, health or fitness data, or payment information — Google Play handles payment details for every purchase in the app, and this app is never told them. There is no chat, no user-generated content and no way for one player to see another.

In-app purchases

Every purchase is optional and is processed entirely by Google Play Billing. This app never sees your card details, billing address or any other payment information — only which product was bought and whether the purchase succeeded.

  • AD-FREE WORKSHOP — a single, one-time payment (not a subscription) that removes the banner advertisement for good. Nothing playable is locked behind it.
  • PIONEER SUPPORTER — a single, one-time payment that removes the banner advertisement too and adds a SUPPORTER plate to your Workshop screen.

A purchase belongs to your Google account: if you reinstall the app or change devices, Google Play remembers it and the app restores it automatically the next time it starts, or when you tap Restore purchases in the Store.

When a purchase completes, the app records a purchase event in Firebase Analytics with the product and the price Google Play showed you, so we can tell which products people find worth buying. It is not linked to your name, your Google account or your payment details.

Why we collect it

  • To fix things. The app reports when something fails; those events should be rare, and are the only way we find out.
  • To decide what to build. Counting which parts of the app people reach tells us what is working.
  • To pay for the app. The banner advertisement, and the optional purchases, are how the app earns money, so that it can be free to play.
  • To complete a purchase where you make one.
  • Nothing is used to make any decision about you as an individual.

Who it is shared with

Google, as the operator of Firebase Analytics, AdMob and Google Play Billing (purchases), processes this data. We do not sell it, and we do not share it with anyone else — no advertising networks other than Google AdMob and AppLovin (see Advertising and your ad choices), no data brokers and no other analytics providers.

Your choices

Consent in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland

If your device is in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app shows Google’s consent form when it first starts, and respects your answer. Saying no is a complete answer: the app plays exactly the same.

Advertising ID and personalised ads

You control your advertising ID in Android itself: Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path varies by device). There you can delete your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation. Adverts will still appear, but they will not be based on your profile.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes your saved progress from the device entirely and resets the anonymous identifier used for statistics.

Your rights

Your legal rights, region by region, and where to complain are set out in Your privacy rights, wherever you live.

Where your progress is kept

Your progress and settings are stored in the app’s own private storage on your device. They are never uploaded, are not backed up to any server of ours, and no other app can read them. Android’s own cloud backup for this app is switched off.

Security

Everything the app sends is encrypted in transit (HTTPS, by the Firebase and AdMob SDKs). The app requests only these permissions: INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE (so the analytics and advertising SDKs can reach the network), VIBRATE (haptic feedback), AD_ID (the advertising ID, for the banner advertisement) and com.android.vending.BILLING (Google Play purchases in the Store). It requests nothing else.

Advertising and your ad choices

Pipeline Pioneers shows advertising to stay free to play. This section applies to every advertisement in the app, from every network.

How it works:

  • Who serves the ads. Google AdMob, which may also fill an ad slot through mediation with AppLovin (AppLovin MAX) and the ad networks they work with. Ad content is limited to a general-audience rating.
  • What an ad network receives. Your device's advertising ID (a resettable identifier Android provides; the app declares the AD_ID permission for it), your IP address (from which the network infers an approximate location such as your country or city), technical information about your device and the app, and how each ad performed — whether it loaded, was shown or was tapped. We never give an ad network your name, email address, contacts, precise location or anything you type.
  • The advertising ID is kept apart from analytics. It is read for advertising only and is never joined to the app's own statistics.
  • Europe, the UK and Switzerland: consent first. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the app shows Google's consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform, a Google-certified consent management platform that uses the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework) before any ad is requested. No ad is requested and the advertising ID is not read until you have answered. Ads are personalised only if you agree to personalisation; otherwise any ad you see is non-personalised, chosen from the app and the screen it appears on.
  • United States: opt out of targeted advertising. Where a US state privacy law gives you the right to opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information or of targeted advertising, Google's privacy form offers that choice; see US state privacy notice.
  • Change your answer in the app at any time: Settings → Privacy & data reopens Google's privacy form. It is shown wherever Google's form applies to you.
  • Anywhere in the world you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your phone's own settings (usually Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads). Ads still appear, but they are not based on a profile of you.
  • Remove ads. The AD-FREE WORKSHOP purchase switches advertising off for good, on every network, and Google Play restores it if you reinstall on the same Google account.
  • Children. No personalised advertising is shown to anyone we know to be under 13.

Each network uses the information under its own privacy policy: How Google uses information from apps that use its services · Google Privacy Policy · AppLovin Privacy Policy.

  • Emails you send us are kept only as long as we need them to answer you, and deleted on request.
