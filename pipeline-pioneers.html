Privacy Policy — Pipeline Pioneers

Last updated: 24 September 2026 · Package com.bilalsafdar.pipelinepioneers

Pipeline Pioneers (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a puzzle game for Android in which you build a CPU from a single logic gate up to a program you wrote. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, who it is shared with, and what control you have over it.

The short version.
  • There is no account. You never give us a name, an email address or a phone number, because there is nowhere to type one.
  • The app shows one banner advertisement on the menu screens (never inside a lab), served by Google AdMob. There are no full-screen or video adverts.
  • There are no in-app purchases. No real money changes hands anywhere in the app.
  • The app collects anonymous usage statistics through Google Firebase Analytics.
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google’s consent form asks you before advertising or statistics are switched on.
  • Your saved progress lives on your device. It is never uploaded.

What the app collects

Anonymous usage statistics (Firebase Analytics)

The app reports events describing what happens in it — a level opened, a circuit completed, a chip unlocked, a setting changed, or an error — so that we can tell which parts people enjoy and which parts are broken.

What is sentExample
Events and their parametersthe screen opened, how long a session lasted, an error
The app version and platformthe version number, Android
An anonymous app instance identifiergenerated by the Firebase SDK; not linked to your name, your Google account or your advertising ID
Coarse information Google’s SDK addscountry, language, device model, OS version, and the fact that the app was opened

The app is set so that Firebase Analytics does not collect your advertising ID.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

To show and measure the banner advertisement, Google AdMob receives:

  • your device’s advertising ID (the app declares the AD_ID permission for this);
  • your IP address, from which Google infers an approximate location such as your country or city;
  • technical information about your device and how the advertisement performed (whether it loaded, was shown or was tapped).

Depending on your settings and where you live, Google may use this to show advertising that is relevant to you. Advertising content is limited to a general-audience rating.

What the app does not collect

No name, email address, phone number, postal address, contacts, calendar, photos, files, microphone, camera, precise location, browsing or search history, list of installed apps, health or fitness data, or payment information. There is no chat, no user-generated content and no way for one player to see another.

Why we collect it

  • To fix things. The app reports when something fails; those events should be rare, and are the only way we find out.
  • To decide what to build. Counting which parts of the app people reach tells us what is working.
  • To pay for the app. The banner advertisement is how the app earns money, so that it can be free to play.
  • Nothing is used to make any decision about you as an individual.

Who it is shared with

Google, as the operator of Firebase Analytics and AdMob, processes this data. We do not sell it, and we do not share it with anyone else — no other advertising networks, data brokers or analytics providers.

Your choices

Consent in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland

If your device is in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app shows Google’s consent form when it first starts, and respects your answer. Saying no is a complete answer: the app plays exactly the same.

Advertising ID and personalised ads

You control your advertising ID in Android itself: Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path varies by device). There you can delete your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation. Adverts will still appear, but they will not be based on your profile.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes your saved progress from the device entirely and resets the anonymous identifier used for statistics.

Your rights

Where the GDPR, the UK GDPR, the Swiss FADP, the CCPA/CPRA or a comparable law applies to you, you have rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection and portability. Because the app holds no information that identifies you, we cannot look up “your” data — there is nothing to look it up by. If you would like to ask anything, write to the address at the bottom of this page.

Where your progress is kept

Your progress and settings are stored in the app’s own private storage on your device. They are never uploaded, are not backed up to any server of ours, and no other app can read them. Android’s own cloud backup for this app is switched off.

Children

Pipeline Pioneers is not directed at children under 13 and is not part of Google Play’s Designed for Families programme. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will act on it — although, as above, the app holds nothing that identifies any individual.

Security

Everything the app sends is encrypted in transit (HTTPS, by the Firebase and AdMob SDKs). The app requests only these permissions: INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE (so the analytics and advertising SDKs can reach the network), VIBRATE (haptic feedback) and AD_ID (the advertising ID, for the banner advertisement). It requests nothing else.

Data retention

Firebase Analytics event data is retained for 14 months, the Firebase default, after which Google deletes it automatically. Aggregate reports (totals and counts, with no identifier attached) may be kept longer.

Changes to this policy

If what the app collects ever changes materially, this page is updated and the date at the top changes with it.

Delete your data

In the app: open Settings → Privacy & data and tap Delete my data. It erases your progress and settings, resets the anonymous analytics identifier and your advertising consent, on the spot.

What it cannot delete: analytics already sent is deleted by Google after 14 months, or earlier on request by email.

By email: if you cannot open the app, or you want Google's analytics copy removed as well, email bilalsafdar3014@gmail.com with the subject “Pipeline Pioneers data deletion”. We answer within 30 days. We will never ask for a password.

Uninstalling the app also deletes everything it stored on your device.
