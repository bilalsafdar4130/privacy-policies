Privacy Policy — BunyadIlm

Last updated: 24 September 2026 · Package com.bunyadilm.app

BunyadIlm (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is an offline guide to education systems, study-abroad pathways and careers, developed and published by Enigvra Studio. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it goes, and how you delete it.

The short version.
  • The app works completely offline. It does not even have permission to use the internet.
  • There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • Your answers, your generated path and your saved items stay on your device only, and are not backed up to the cloud.
  • You can delete all of it at any time from inside the app.

What the app stores, on your device only

InformationWhy
Your onboarding answers (for example your country, current education level, interests and date of birth)To build a path and guidance that fit you, and to show a guardian note to users aged 13–17.
Your generated path, saved plans, favourites and quiz resultsSo they are still there next time you open the app.
A profile picture, only if you choose oneCopied into the app’s private storage and shown on your profile. The original in your gallery is not changed.
Your settings (for example light or dark theme)To remember your choices.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. The app has no internet permission, and Android cloud backup is switched off for it, so the data does not leave your device.

Links to other websites

The app links to official sources — regulators, examination boards, universities and scholarship providers. Tapping a link opens it in your browser, outside the app; those websites have their own privacy policies. Sharing the app uses Android’s share sheet, and only shares the text you see.

What we never collect

No name or email is required, and nothing is uploaded: no contacts, no location, no advertising ID, no analytics and no crash reports.

Children and guardians

BunyadIlm is intended for students aged 13 and older. Users aged 13–17 see a note suggesting they involve a parent or guardian. Children under 13 should not use the app.

Accuracy of the guidance

Education data ships inside the app and is verified each release, but deadlines, fees and eligibility change. Always confirm on the official website before you act.

Delete your data

In the app: open Settings → Privacy policy and tap Delete my data (or Settings → Reset & clear all data). It erases your answers, generated path, saved plans, favourites, quiz results, profile picture and settings, immediately and permanently.

What it cannot delete: nothing — BunyadIlm has no server and holds no copy of your data anywhere else.

By email: if you cannot open the app, email bilalsafdar3014@gmail.com with the subject “BunyadIlm data deletion”. Because we hold no copy of your data, the answer will explain how to remove it from your device; we reply within 30 days and will never ask for a password.

Uninstalling the app also deletes everything it stored on your device.

Changes to this policy

If what the app collects ever changes — for example if it ever adds analytics, advertising or an online service — this page is updated before that version is released, and the date at the top changes with it.
