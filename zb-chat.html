Privacy Policy — ZB Chat

Last updated: 1 October 2026 · Package com.zbchat.app

ZB Chat (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a private messenger that connects exactly two phones, developed and published by Enigvra Studio. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it goes, and how you delete it.

The short version.
  • There is no ZB Chat server. Messages go directly from your phone to your partner’s phone, over local Wi‑Fi, a hotspot or Bluetooth.
  • Every message, voice note, photo and file is end-to-end encrypted. We cannot read any of it, because it never passes through us.
  • Your account, your keys and your chat history are stored only on your phone, encrypted, and are never backed up to the cloud.
  • There is no analytics and no tracking of any kind, and this version shows no advertising. Future versions may show ads from Google AdMob or AppLovin — see Advertising.
  • Camera, microphone and location are used only when you choose to share them with your partner, and there is always a visible indicator while they are on.

Permissions

  • Nearby Wi‑Fi devices and Bluetooth — to find and connect to your partner’s phone directly. They are not used to locate you.
  • Camera and microphone — for photos, voice notes, and live camera or voice that you start or allow. Never in the background without a visible indicator.
  • Location, including in the background — only while you have turned on live location sharing, so your partner can see you are safe. A notification is shown the whole time, and you can stop at any moment.
  • Notifications — to tell you a message arrived. Notifications never contain the message text.

Maps

When you open the location map, map pictures are downloaded from OpenStreetMap’s tile servers. Like any website, those servers see your phone’s IP address and which area of the map was requested; they do not see your messages, your identity or your partner. Their use of that information is covered by the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy.

What we never collect

No contacts, no call logs, no SMS, no advertising ID, no analytics, no crash reports sent to us, and no copy of any message. There is no account on any server — nothing to breach.

Security

Messages use X25519 key agreement, HKDF and AES‑256‑GCM. Wi‑Fi peers must prove they hold your partner’s pinned key before they can connect. The app blocks screenshots, can require your fingerprint or device lock to open, and disables Android cloud backup so your chats never leave the two phones.

Advertising

This version of ZB Chat shows no advertising and contains no advertising SDK. Future versions may show advertising served by Google AdMob or AppLovin (AppLovin MAX). If one does, the network serving an ad would receive your device’s advertising ID, your IP address (from which it infers an approximate location), technical information about your device and how the ad performed; in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland no ad would be requested until you had answered a consent form; and this page would be updated, with the date at the top, before that version is released.

Children

ZB Chat is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly allow them to use it.

Delete your data

In the app: open Settings → Privacy policy & delete my data and tap Delete my data (the same action is Erase account and start over in Settings). It destroys your account, your encryption keys, your pairing and every message and file stored on your phone, immediately and permanently.

What it cannot delete: copies of messages you already sent that are on your partner’s phone. Those are on their device, under their control — use the app’s delete or revoke options before erasing, or ask your partner to clear the chat.

By email: if you cannot open the app, email bilalsafdar3014@gmail.com with the subject “ZB Chat data deletion”. Because we hold no copy of your data, the answer will explain how to remove it from your device; we reply within 30 days and will never ask for a password.

Uninstalling the app also deletes everything it stored on your device.

Changes to this policy

If what the app collects ever changes — for example if it ever adds analytics, advertising or an online service — this page is updated before that version is released, and the date at the top changes with it.
