Privacy Policy — ZB Chat

Last updated: 8 October 2026 · Package com.zbchat.app

ZB Chat (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a private messenger that connects exactly two phones, developed and published by Enigvra Studio. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it goes, and how you delete it.

The short version.
  • There is no ZB Chat server. Messages go directly from your phone to your partner’s phone, over local Wi‑Fi, a hotspot or Bluetooth.
  • Every message, voice note, photo and file is end-to-end encrypted. We cannot read any of it, because it never passes through us.
  • Your account, your keys and your chat history are stored only on your phone, encrypted, and are never backed up to the cloud.
  • There is no analytics and no tracking of any kind, and this version shows no advertising. Future versions may show ads from Google AdMob or AppLovin — see Advertising and your ad choices.
  • Camera, microphone and location are used only when you choose to share them with your partner, and there is always a visible indicator while they are on.

Permissions

  • Nearby Wi‑Fi devices and Bluetooth — to find and connect to your partner’s phone directly. They are not used to locate you.
  • Camera and microphone — for photos, voice notes, and live camera or voice that you start or allow. Never in the background without a visible indicator.
  • Location, including in the background — only while you have turned on live location sharing, so your partner can see you are safe. A notification is shown the whole time, and you can stop at any moment.
  • Notifications — to tell you a message arrived. Notifications never contain the message text.

Maps

When you open the location map, map pictures are downloaded from OpenStreetMap’s tile servers. Like any website, those servers see your phone’s IP address and which area of the map was requested; they do not see your messages, your identity or your partner. Their use of that information is covered by the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy.

What we never collect

No contacts, no call logs, no SMS, no advertising ID, no analytics, no crash reports sent to us, and no copy of any message. There is no account on any server — nothing to breach.

Security

Messages use X25519 key agreement, HKDF and AES‑256‑GCM. Wi‑Fi peers must prove they hold your partner’s pinned key before they can connect. The app blocks screenshots, can require your fingerprint or device lock to open, and disables Android cloud backup so your chats never leave the two phones.

Advertising and your ad choices

This version of ZB Chat shows no advertising and contains no advertising SDK. Enigvra Studio plans to support its apps with advertising, so this section already describes how advertising works in any version of ZB Chat that shows it. That version will not be released until this page names it, with the date at the top updated.

In a version that shows advertising:

  • Who serves the ads. Google AdMob, which may also fill an ad slot through mediation with AppLovin (AppLovin MAX) and the ad networks they work with. Ad content is limited to a general-audience rating.
  • What an ad network receives. Your device's advertising ID (a resettable identifier Android provides; the app declares the AD_ID permission for it), your IP address (from which the network infers an approximate location such as your country or city), technical information about your device and the app, and how each ad performed — whether it loaded, was shown or was tapped. We never give an ad network your name, email address, contacts, precise location or anything you type.
  • The advertising ID is kept apart from analytics. It is read for advertising only and is never joined to the app's own statistics.
  • Europe, the UK and Switzerland: consent first. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the app shows Google's consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform, a Google-certified consent management platform that uses the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework) before any ad is requested. No ad is requested and the advertising ID is not read until you have answered. Ads are personalised only if you agree to personalisation; otherwise any ad you see is non-personalised, chosen from the app and the screen it appears on.
  • United States: opt out of targeted advertising. Where a US state privacy law gives you the right to opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information or of targeted advertising, Google's privacy form offers that choice; see US state privacy notice.
  • Change your answer in the app at any time, from the app's privacy settings, wherever Google's form applies to you.
  • Anywhere in the world you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your phone's own settings (usually Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads). Ads still appear, but they are not based on a profile of you.
  • Children. No personalised advertising is shown to anyone we know to be under 13.

Each network uses the information under its own privacy policy: How Google uses information from apps that use its services · Google Privacy Policy · AppLovin Privacy Policy.

Transfers from the EEA, the UK and Switzerland rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework (and its UK and Swiss extensions) where the recipient is certified under it, and otherwise on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or an equivalent safeguard. Enigvra Studio itself runs no server that receives your data.

How long data is kept

  • On your device: until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app.
  • Advertising data is kept by each ad network under its own retention policy. Google, for example, anonymises advertising data in its logs by removing part of the IP address after 9 months and cookie information after 18 months.
  • Emails you send us are kept only as long as we need them to answer you, and deleted on request.

Delete your data

In the app: open Settings → Privacy policy & delete my data and tap Delete my data (the same action is Erase account and start over in Settings). It destroys your account, your encryption keys, your pairing and every message and file stored on your phone, immediately and permanently.

What it cannot delete: copies of messages you already sent that are on your partner’s phone. Those are on their device, under their control — use the app’s delete or revoke options before erasing, or ask your partner to clear the chat.

By email: if you cannot open the app, email bilalsafdar3014@gmail.com with the subject “ZB Chat data deletion”. Because we hold no copy of your data, the answer will explain how to remove it from your device; we reply within 30 days and will never ask for a password.

Uninstalling the app also deletes everything it stored on your device.
