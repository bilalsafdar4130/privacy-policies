Privacy Policy — SparkLogic

Last updated: 15 August 2026 · Package com.bilalsafdar.sparklogic

SparkLogic (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a puzzle game for Android published by Bilal Safdar. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, who it is shared with, and what control you have over it.

The short version
  • The app has no accounts and no login. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number or any other personal detail.
  • Your game progress is stored only on your device.
  • The app uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is played, and Google AdMob to show two banner advertisements.
  • Those two Google services collect device identifiers, including your device’s advertising ID, and technical information about your device.
  • The app requests no sensitive permissions: no location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files or calendar.

What information is collected

1. Information you give us

None. There is no registration, no profile, no comment box, no chat, and no way to submit text or files to us through the app.

2. Information stored on your device only

Your game progress is written to a save file in the app’s private storage on your device. It contains your level progress, star ratings, in-game currency balances, cosmetic purchases, daily mission progress and your settings.

This file is never transmitted to us or to anyone else. It is not backed up to the cloud — the app explicitly disables Android’s automatic backup, so it does not leave your device at all. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

3. Information collected automatically by Google services

The app includes two Google software development kits (SDKs). Each collects data according to Google’s own policies, not ours.

Google Firebase Analytics — used to understand how the game is played, so we can tell which levels are too hard, where players stop, and whether features are used. It records events such as: starting and finishing a level (including how many moves and how long it took), completing the tutorial, using a hint, unlocking an achievement, claiming a daily reward, earning or spending in-game currency, making a purchase, and technical failures.

Alongside these events, Firebase collects a randomly generated app instance identifier, your device model, operating system version, language, and an approximate region derived from your IP address.

Google AdMob — used to show advertisements. AdMob collects your device’s advertising ID and technical information about your device in order to select and measure ads.

None of the events the app sends contain your name, email address, or any text you have written, because the app never collects any of those things.

We do not sell your information. We do not build advertising profiles ourselves, and we do not receive any personally identifying information about you from Google.

Advertising

SparkLogic shows two banner advertisements: one on the home screen and one on the shop screen. There are no full-screen adverts, no video adverts, and no adverts on any puzzle board. No part of the game is locked behind watching an advert.

Advertisements are provided by Google AdMob. Google may use your device’s advertising ID to show adverts that are relevant to you.

You can control this on your device. In Android Settings ▸ Privacy ▸ Ads, you can delete your advertising ID or opt out of personalised advertising. Doing so does not stop adverts appearing, but they will no longer be personalised.

Google’s own policy on how it uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

In-app purchases

The app offers optional in-app purchases for in-game currency and a supporter pack. All payments are processed by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment card details, billing address, or any other financial information — Google handles the transaction entirely and only tells the app whether a purchase succeeded.

Nothing that affects gameplay is locked behind a purchase. Every in-game currency can be earned by playing.

Children

SparkLogic is not directed at children under 13. Its target audience in the Google Play Console is set to 13 and over, and it is not enrolled in Google Play’s Designed for Families programme.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact us at the address below and we will act on it. In practice, the app collects no personal information from anyone.

Your choices and your rights

  • Delete your game data. Open Settings ▸ RESET PROGRESS inside the app to erase all local progress. Uninstalling the app also removes it completely.
  • Limit ad tracking. Use Android Settings ▸ Privacy ▸ Ads to reset or delete your advertising ID, or to opt out of personalised advertising.
  • Ask us about your data. There is no account to look up, so we hold nothing that identifies you personally. If you are in a region with data protection rights (such as the UK/EU under GDPR, or California under the CCPA) and wish to make a request about data held by Google’s services, contact us and we will help where we can.

Because the app has no accounts, we cannot identify an individual user’s data, and there is therefore nothing for us to retrieve or delete on our side.

Data retention

We retain no data ourselves. Analytics and advertising data is retained by Google according to its own retention settings, which are configured to Google’s default period for Firebase Analytics.

Security

Data sent by the Google SDKs is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections. Your save file is stored in the app’s private storage area, which other apps on your device cannot read.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same URL and the “Last updated” date at the top will change. Material changes will also be noted in the app’s release notes.
