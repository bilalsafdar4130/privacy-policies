Privacy Policy — SparkLogic

Last updated: 1 October 2026 · Package com.bilalsafdar.sparklogic

SparkLogic (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a puzzle game for Android published by Enigvra Studio, the trading name of Bilal Safdar. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, who it is shared with, and what control you have over it.

The short version
  • The app has no accounts and no login. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number or any other personal detail.
  • Your game progress is stored only on your device.
  • The app uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is played. You can switch it off, anywhere in the world, in Settings → ABOUT & LEGAL; in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland it stays off until you say yes.
  • This build shows one banner advertisement on menu screens, served by Google AdMob, which reads your device’s advertising ID. You can turn personalisation off, and the AD-FREE FOREVER purchase removes the banner entirely. No advertisement is shown at all in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland, Russia or Belarus.
  • Nothing about you is published to other players. The global ladder — the one feature that ever did — is switched off in this version.
  • Once per launch the app asks our database whether this installation has a temporary support unlock, sending only the six-character support code shown in Settings → ABOUT & LEGAL → DIAGNOSTICS. It identifies the installation, not you.
  • The app requests no sensitive permissions: no location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files or calendar.
  • Advertising is served by Google AdMob, and may also be served through AppLovin — see Advertising partners.

What information is collected

1. Information you give us

None. There is no registration, no profile, no comment box, no chat, and no way to submit text or files to us through the app.

2. Information stored on your device only

Your game progress is written to a save file in the app’s private storage on your device. It contains your level progress, star ratings, in-game currency balances, cosmetic purchases, daily mission progress and your settings.

This file is never transmitted to us or to anyone else. It is not backed up to the cloud — the app explicitly disables Android’s automatic backup, so it does not leave your device at all. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

3. Information collected automatically by Google services

Google Firebase Analytics — used to understand how the game is played, so we can tell which levels are too hard, where players stop, and whether features are used. It records events such as: starting and finishing a level (including how many moves and how long it took), completing the tutorial, using a hint, unlocking an achievement, claiming a daily reward, earning or spending in-game currency, making a purchase, and technical failures.

Alongside these events, Firebase collects a randomly generated app instance identifier, your device model, operating system version, language, and an approximate region derived from your IP address.

You can turn all of it off. Settings → ABOUT & LEGAL → ANALYTICS stops it everywhere in the world, immediately, and the game plays exactly the same afterwards. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland it is off until you say yes: the app asks once, on first launch, treats no answer as a no, and lets you change your mind at any time in the same place.

Analytics does not collect your advertising identifier. Firebase’s advertising-ID collection is switched off in the app’s own manifest rather than merely not used, so the identifier AdMob reads to serve a banner (see Advertising below) is never joined to your gameplay statistics. They are two separate things and the app keeps them that way.

4. The global ladder — switched off in this version

Earlier builds offered an opt-in worldwide high-score list. It is switched off in the version you are running: no handle, no score and no sign-in identifier is created or sent, and nothing about your play is shown to any other player. If a future version brings it back, this policy will be updated before that version ships.

5. The support unlock check

Once per launch, the app asks our Firebase database a single question: whether this installation has been granted a temporary support unlock. It sends only the six-character support code shown in Settings → ABOUT & LEGAL → DIAGNOSTICS. That code is generated at random on this device, it identifies the installation rather than you, and on its own it does nothing at all. Your IP address is visible to Google when the app makes this request, as it would be for any web request.

This check runs whether or not analytics is switched on, because it is what lets us help a specific person who has written in for support.

Nothing the app sends contains your name, email address, or any text you have written, because the app never collects any of those things.

Advertising

This build shows one banner advertisement, on the home screen, the Vault and the Store. Never on a puzzle board, never between you and a level, never something you have to watch, and nothing playable is ever locked behind one. There are no interstitials and no rewarded advertisements.

Advertisements are served by Google AdMob, which reads your device’s advertising ID to do so. That identifier is resettable at any time in Android’s own settings, and it is never joined to the gameplay statistics described above.

Where no advertisement is shown at all:

  • the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland — serving advertisements there requires a Google-certified consent management platform, and this build does not carry one, so it requests no advertisement and reads no advertising identifier;
  • Russia and Belarus, where Google does not serve advertisements to users;
  • to any player Google Play tells the app is a child, anywhere in the world.

Your controls:

  • Settings → ABOUT & LEGAL → ADS switches personalisation off — you still see a banner, but it is contextual rather than targeted;
  • AD-FREE FOREVER, a one-time purchase in the Store, removes the banner permanently;
  • no advertisement is ever personalised for a player under 18, or where the store gives no age signal in a region that expects one.

Google’s own policy on how it uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

In-app purchases

The app offers optional in-app purchases for in-game currency and a supporter pack. All payments are processed by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment card details, billing address, or any other financial information — Google handles the transaction entirely and only tells the app whether a purchase succeeded.

Nothing that affects gameplay is locked behind a purchase. Every in-game currency can be earned by playing.

Children

SparkLogic is not directed at children under 13. Its target audience in the Google Play Console is set to 13 and over, and it is not enrolled in Google Play’s Designed for Families programme.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact us at the address below and we will act on it. In practice, the app collects no personal information from anyone.

Your choices and your rights

  • Switch analytics off, anywhere in the world: Settings → ABOUT & LEGAL → ANALYTICS. It takes effect immediately.
  • Withdraw consent if you gave it: Settings → ABOUT & LEGAL → ADS → PRIVACY CHOICES. Withdrawing is exactly as easy as giving, which is the point.
  • Delete everything: Settings → ABOUT & LEGAL → SAVE DATA & RESET PROGRESS is this game’s delete my data. It erases the local save file, the analytics identifier the events were sent under, and your stored privacy answers. Uninstalling removes the local half as well.
  • Read the rules that apply where you are: Settings → ABOUT & LEGAL → TERMS & LEGAL shows the statutory notices for your own country, and lets you read every other region’s.
  • Ask us about your data. There is no account to look up, so there is nothing that identifies you personally for us to retrieve. If you are in a region with data protection rights — the EEA and UK under the GDPR, Switzerland under the FADP, Brazil under the LGPD, California and other US states, Canada under PIPEDA — write to the address below and we will help where we can.

Where to complain. EEA: your national data protection authority (the list is published by the EDPB). UK: the ICO. Switzerland: the FDPIC. Brazil: the ANPD. Canada: the Office of the Privacy Commissioner.

Data retention

We retain no data ourselves. Analytics and advertising data is retained by Google according to its own retention settings, which are configured to Google’s default period for Firebase Analytics.

Security

Data sent by the Google SDKs is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections. Your save file is stored in the app’s private storage area, which other apps on your device cannot read.

Advertising partners

The advertising described above is served by Google AdMob. The app may also show advertising served through AppLovin (AppLovin MAX), which can fill an ad slot from the networks it works with. Whichever network serves an ad receives the same kind of information AdMob does: your device’s advertising ID, your IP address (from which it infers an approximate location), technical information about your device, and how the ad performed. The same rules apply to every network: where a consent form is required, no ad is requested until you have answered it, and an ad-free purchase removes ads from every network.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same URL and the “Last updated” date at the top will change. Material changes will also be noted in the app’s release notes.

Delete your data

In the app: open Settings → About & legal → Privacy policy and tap Delete my data (the same wipe is Reset progress under Save data & reset progress). It erases your save file and settings, throws away the anonymous analytics identifier and any leaderboard entry, and resets your advertising consent, on the spot.

What it cannot delete: purchases (AD-FREE FOREVER, the Supporter Pack) are held by Google Play against your Google account and stay yours. Analytics already sent is deleted by Google after 14 months, or earlier on request by email.

By email: if you cannot open the app, or you want Google's analytics copy removed as well, email bilalsafdar3014@gmail.com with the subject “SparkLogic data deletion”. We answer within 30 days. We will never ask for a password.

Uninstalling the app also deletes everything it stored on your device.
